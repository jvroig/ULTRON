# Team Charter: Visual Architecture Showcase (`web-viz`)

## Mission & Scope
Design and build a polished, responsive light/dark self-contained ultron-architecture.html accurately depicting ULTRON's as-implemented architecture.

## Organization Position
- **Team Slug:** `web-viz`
- **Parent Team:** `docs`
- **Hive Path:** `ultron/hive/docs/teams/web-viz`

## Team Operating Principles
1. **Flat Intra-Team Collaboration:** Roles within `web-viz` are specialist peers. `web-viz/lead` coordinates work inside this team and handles all external communication with parent/child teams and `ultron-prime`.
2. **Board Visibility Discipline:**
   - Log role-specific technical lessons that only benefit your future sessions as `--visibility personal`.
   - Log tasks, reviews, and team-shared lessons as `--visibility team` (add `--with-team <other-team>` when coordinating across parent/child team boundaries).
   - Log repository-wide architectural announcements or universal lessons as `--visibility global`.
3. **Reactive Doorbelling:** After posting or replying on the ULTRON board for another agent, always resolve their doorbell address (`whois <agent_id> --id-only`) and doorbell them as your role assignment describes.
4. **No Rogue Provisioning:** Only `ultron-prime` creates teams/roles or enables/disables agent sessions. Escalate headcount or sub-team activation needs up the chain of command.
