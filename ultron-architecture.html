Core concepts

1. ULTRON PRIME leads

  • The User talks only to ULTRON PRIME.
  • ULTRON PRIME manages the entire network beneath it: all agents, teams and the hierarchy.
  • It can spawn and direct agents and teams as needed.

2. Teams are the unit of work

  • Agents work in teams; teams are the core design philosophy, not an add-on.
  • Teams relate to one another through the hierarchy.

3. Tiered roles

  • Each agent has a role, and roles are tiered (e.g., team leads above team members).

4. Layered hierarchy with escalation

  • Teams are arranged like a corporate org chart: a team can be above, beside or below another team, and branches can go to different depths.
  • Each team reports to the team above it. Work and questions escalate up the chain, ultimately to ULTRON PRIME and, when needed, to the User.

5. Central message board

  • Coordination runs through a shared message board, inspired by the OpenAI / Hugging Face hack.

6. Universal

  • Built as a general framework, not for one domain. Use cases plug in as teams.
